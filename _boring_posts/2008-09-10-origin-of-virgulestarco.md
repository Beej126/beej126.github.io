date: 2008-09-10

---
I'm drafting off the idea of expanding geeky punctuation into words (popularized by [www.SlashDot.org][1])   
([Funny story behind his originating motivation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slashdot))   
so SlashDot equals /. 

Based on that, being a comment happy developer, I thought an expansion of /* would be particularly apropos as that's the well known opening [comment syntax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comment_\(computer_programming\)) in languages like C, C++, C#, Java, T-SQL, etc. 

Of course, SlashStar.com and readily desirable derivates were already taken 🙁 … however, browsing for slash synonyms turned up "[virgule](https://dictionary.reference.com/browse/virgule)", specifically for the <u>forward</u> slash character, nice!... yeah ok, it doesn't role off the tongue but its obscurity meant I had an available domain name hit and that's obviously key! 🙂 

And thus www.VirguleStar.com was born.

 [1]: https://www.SlashDot.org
